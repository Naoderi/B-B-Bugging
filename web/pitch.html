Relevance and impact

Protect money needed for rent

A student with a £2,000 fictional balance asks for 100% annual growth and wants to explore leverage. The same funds might cover next month’s rent.

Campus Index checks essential spending, emergency runway, investment horizon and loss tolerance before it loads prices.

Live safety veto

No suitable allocation

The essential-expenses rule stops the workflow before it prepares an allocation. The audit records the refusal.

02 / 05

Traceable reasoning and oversight

An agent that cites, and a guardian that checks

01The ASI:One coach quotes the student’s own words into the profile. It asks rather than guesses about rent money, cash runway and horizon.
02It calls the verified engine and what-ifs as tools. Every £ and % in its answer cites a fact id.
03A separate Risk Guardian uAgent reruns everything, matches the digest and rejects ungrounded figures. Only the student can approve.

ETF trace shows dated price inputs, arithmetic, the applicable rule and its result. The agent can skip unsafe work and explain why. The audit keeps every completed tool call and paper action.
